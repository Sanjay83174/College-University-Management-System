Admissions at North Gate University

Admissions Process

  1. Register online and fill out the application form.
  2. Upload required documents and pay the application fee.
  3. Appear for the entrance exam (if applicable).
  4. Attend counseling/interview (if shortlisted).
  5. Receive admission offer and confirm your seat.
Apply Now

Eligibility Criteria

  • Undergraduate (Engineering): 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics; minimum 60% aggregate.
  • Undergraduate (Degree): 10+2 in relevant stream; minimum 50% aggregate.
  • Postgraduate: Bachelor’s degree in relevant field; minimum 55% aggregate.
  • Ph.D.: Master’s degree with minimum 60% aggregate and entrance test/interview.

Required Documents

  • 10th & 12th/PUC mark sheets
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Conduct Certificate
  • Entrance Exam Score Card (if applicable)
  • Passport size photographs
  • Photo ID proof (Aadhaar/Passport/Driving License)
  • Caste/Category certificate (if applicable)
