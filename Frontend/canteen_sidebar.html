Canteen at North Gate University

Enjoy delicious, healthy, and affordable meals in a vibrant and hygienic environment. Our canteen is the heart of campus life!

Canteen Facilities

  • Spacious dining area with modern seating
  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian options
  • Snacks, beverages, and bakery counter
  • Free Wi-Fi zone
  • Fresh fruit and salad bar

Chinese

Noodles, Fried Rice, Manchurian

Snacks & Beverages

Sandwiches, Samosa, Tea, Coffee, Juices

Hygiene & Quality Standards

  • Regular kitchen inspections and quality checks
  • Use of fresh and high-quality ingredients
  • Trained and courteous staff
  • Clean and sanitized dining environment
