About the Library

The North Gate University Library is a hub of knowledge and learning, offering a vast collection of books, journals, e-resources, and digital content. Our library is open to all students, faculty, and staff, providing a quiet and resourceful environment for study and research.

  • Over 50,000 books and reference materials
  • Access to 200+ national and international journals
  • Digital library with e-books and e-journals
  • Dedicated reading rooms and group study areas
  • Online catalog and book reservation system

Library Services

  • Book lending and return
  • Reference and research assistance
  • Inter-library loan
  • Photocopying and printing
  • Workshops and training sessions

Timings

Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM
Sunday: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Rules

  • Maintain silence at all times
  • No food or drinks allowed
  • Return books on or before the due date
  • Handle books and resources with care

Digital Library & Gallery

  • Access e-books and e-journals via the Digital Library Portal
  • Online video lectures and tutorials
  • Research databases and archives

Frequently Asked Questions

Fill out the library membership form at the circulation desk with your student/staff ID.

Students can borrow up to 3 books, faculty up to 5 books at a time.

Log in to the Digital Library Portal using your university credentials.
