Placements at North Gate University

Placement Cell Overview

The Placement Cell at North Gate University is dedicated to providing students with the best career opportunities. Our team works closely with top recruiters, organizes training sessions, and supports students throughout the placement process.

Placement Process

  1. Pre-placement training and skill development
  2. Registration for campus placements
  3. Company presentations and pre-placement talks
  4. Written tests, group discussions, and interviews
  5. Offer letters and joining formalities
