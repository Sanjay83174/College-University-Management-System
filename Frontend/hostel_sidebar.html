Hostel Life at North Gate University

Experience a safe, comfortable, and vibrant residential life. Our hostels are designed to provide a home away from home for students from across the country and the world.

Hostel Facilities

  • Spacious and well-furnished rooms
  • High-speed Wi-Fi connectivity
  • Modern dining hall with nutritious meals
  • 24/7 security and CCTV surveillance
  • Laundry and housekeeping services
  • Recreation rooms and sports facilities

Room Types

Single Occupancy

Private room for individual comfort and privacy.

Double Occupancy

Shared room for two students, ideal for making friends.

Family Accommodation

Limited family quarters for married students or visiting parents.

Hostel Rules

  • Maintain discipline and respect quiet hours.
  • No smoking, alcohol, or illegal substances allowed.
  • Visitors are allowed only during designated hours with prior permission.
  • Keep rooms and common areas clean and tidy.
  • Report any maintenance issues promptly to the warden.
