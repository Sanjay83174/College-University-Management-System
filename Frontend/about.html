About North Gate University

Who We Are

North Gate University is a leading institution of higher learning, renowned for its commitment to academic excellence, research, and holistic student development. Established in 1995, our university has grown into a vibrant community of scholars, innovators, and leaders. We offer a diverse range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, sciences, business, humanities, and more.

  • Over 10,000 students from across the country and abroad
  • Highly qualified faculty with global exposure
  • Modern campus with world-class infrastructure
  • Strong industry connections and placement support
  • Inclusive, safe, and dynamic campus life

Our Mission & Vision
