Scholarships at North Gate University

Empowering dreams through financial support. We offer a range of scholarships to help talented and deserving students achieve their academic goals.

Types of Scholarships

Merit-based

For outstanding academic achievers

Need-based

For students from economically weaker backgrounds

Sports Scholarships

For excellence in sports and athletics

Special Category

For differently-abled, minorities, and more

Eligibility Criteria

  • Consistent academic performance
  • Proof of income (for need-based scholarships)
  • Participation in sports/cultural activities (for relevant scholarships)
  • Valid government-issued documents

Application Process

  1. Check eligibility and select the scholarship
  2. Fill out the online application form
  3. Upload required documents
  4. Appear for interview (if applicable)
  5. Await selection and award notification
